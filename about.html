ABOUT

I'm a 24-year-old Multimedia Design student based in Buenos Aires, AR, with a passion for bringing ideas to life through impactful visuals. Over the past few years, I’ve worked with companies based in Argentina, the United States, and the United Kingdom, allowing me to grow within international teams and adapt to a variety of creative environments and project needs. My main focus lies in motion graphics and graphic design, where I blend creativity, technical precision, and storytelling to craft unique visual experiences. I'm driven by curiosity and constantly looking for new ways to evolve. I see design as a living process: always shifting, always growing. My goal is to not only meet expectations but to elevate every project with thoughtful design solutions that connect and resonate.
